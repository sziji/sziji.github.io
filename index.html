Ziji Sheng

Master’s student · Huazhong University of Science and Technology

I am a master’s student at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), expecting to graduate in June 2027. I received my Bachelor of Engineering from Wuhan University in 2024. I am advised by Prof. Pan Zhou, with additional research guidance from Prof. Daizong Liu.

My research centers on the safety, trustworthiness, and applications of multimodal large language models and multi-agent systems. My work spans LLM privacy, evidence-grounded collaborative reasoning and planning, and multimodal hallucination mitigation. I am interested in making model judgments and actions both reliable and understandable.

I enjoy interdisciplinary research and connecting academic ideas with practical applications. I am particularly interested in multimodal models, embodied intelligence, and world models, and am currently exploring uncertainty-aware learning for vision–language–action (VLA) models.

NeurIPS 2026Accepted · Main Track

Object Hallucination Mitigation in Large Vision-Language Models via Self-Vision Dual Masking and Uncertainty-Triggered Assembly

Ziji Sheng*, Guiyao Tie*, Weidong Wang*, Jiawen Shi*, Junhao Dong, Xiaoye Qu, Shanshan Ye, Daizong Liu, Dengpan Ye, Pan Zhou, Jing Zhang

SIGMA is a training-free framework that mitigates object hallucinations using the model’s own vision encoder. Supporting and complementary masks distinguish useful visual evidence from potentially misleading context; uncertainty-triggered assembly activates correction as needed, without an external vision expert.

Multimodal LLMs · Visual grounding · Uncertainty
ACL 2026Main Conference · Long Paper

LEARNERCOMPASS: Intelligent Tutoring System with Dynamic Cognitive Diagnosis and Multi-Model Path Planning

Ziji Sheng*, Guiyao Tie*, Weidong Wang, Pan Zhou, Daizong Liu

Combines dynamic knowledge graphs, Graph-RAG, multi-agent cognitive diagnosis, and multi-model path search to ground collaborative reasoning and adaptive planning in structured evidence. Personalized education provides an application setting for studying reliable reasoning and planning.

Merger-as-a-Stealer: Stealing Targeted PII from Aligned LLMs with Model Merging

Lin Lu*, Zhigang Zuo*, Ziji Sheng*, Pan Zhou

Reveals a privacy vulnerability in model merging: malicious model parameters can undermine the privacy-protective behavior of aligned LLMs and enable targeted extraction of personal information. The study evaluates this risk across models and merging methods.
